A source-linked vendor due diligence case for reviewing a fictional payroll service, assessing evidence and controls, tracking findings in a risk register, and preparing a human decision.

All supplier, service, evidence, dates, roles, risk ratings and outcomes are synthetic. The materials demonstrate a repeatable third-party risk management (TPRM) workflow; they are not real assurance evidence, a completed client engagement, or approval to onboard.

How to use this packet

  1. Start with the vendor risk assessment and follow every conclusion to its evidence item.
  2. Use the methodology and questionnaire to separate missing, stale, out-of-scope and unverified evidence.
  3. Review finding owners, due dates, risk ratings and follow-up actions in the risk register export. The ratings remain subject to reviewer judgment.
  4. Read the decision brief and scorecard as simulated outputs. Only an authorized reviewer can accept risk or approve onboarding.
  5. Use the feedback record after a real peer or mentor discussion; do not imply feedback occurred before it is recorded.
