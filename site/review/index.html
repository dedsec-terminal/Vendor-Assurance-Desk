How to use this packet

  1. Start with the supplier review and follow every conclusion to its evidence item.
  2. Use the methodology and questionnaire to separate missing, stale, out-of-scope and unverified evidence.
  3. Read the decision brief and scorecard as simulated outputs. Only an authorized reviewer can accept risk or approve onboarding.
  4. Use the feedback record after a real peer or mentor discussion; do not imply feedback occurred before it is recorded.
