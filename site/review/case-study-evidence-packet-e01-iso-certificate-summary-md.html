Fictional supplier: HarborPay Demo
Claimed standard/version: ISO/IEC 27001:2022
Claimed certification period: 1 November 2025–31 October 2026
Claimed scope: “Information security management system supporting HarborPay Demo payroll processing SaaS.”
Issuer, accreditation body, certificate number, signature and verification URL: Not supplied.

This is a constructed summary of a certificate claim, not a certificate image and not an independently authenticated record. The entity name and scope wording are hypothetical. No certification body or accreditation status is asserted.

Reviewer analysis

The stated expiry precedes the assessment date (16 November 2026), so E01 cannot establish current certification on the review date. Even within its stated dates, reliance would require checking the certificate against the issuing body's authoritative directory, confirming the legal entity and relevant locations, reading the exact scope and exclusions, and checking the issuer/accreditation chain and certificate status. A standard name alone does not establish that payroll processing, the relevant hosting boundary, or every subcontractor was covered.

Treat CERT-EXPIRY as an assurance freshness and authenticity gap. Expiry or missing verification is not evidence that a security control failed, that a breach occurred, or that the supplier is unsafe. Request current independently verifiable evidence and preserve this original record; do not silently replace its dates.
