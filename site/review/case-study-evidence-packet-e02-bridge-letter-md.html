E02-B — management bridge-letter scenario (synthetic)

Source: case-study/evidence-packet/E02_bridge_letter.md166 words · SHA-256 96f69a5a1c036d7b…
Training fixture only. HarborPay Demo, evidence, dates, roles, and findings are fictional. This packet supports a simulated human review; it is not a real vendor decision or assurance opinion.

Fictional authoring role: HarborPay Demo management. No real supplier communication exists.

For this exercise, assume management states that no material changes to the payroll service control environment came to its attention between 1 October and 16 November 2026, while directing customers to the prior-period assurance report. No signed letter, responsible executive, incident log, change inventory, or corroborating records are supplied; this is a scenario prompt, not evidence that management actually made the statement.

Review treatment

A bridge letter is management representation for a gap after a report period. It does not extend the independent examination period, test controls during the gap, establish that exceptions were remediated, or substitute for a current report. Evaluate the named signatory's authority, exact dates and service boundary, meaning of “material change,” security incidents, significant control changes and subprocessor changes; corroborate with appropriate records where available. Record the interval after 30 September as an assurance limitation until verified. Do not treat this hypothetical statement as evidence of no incidents or no change.
