Training fixture only. HarborPay Demo, evidence, dates, roles, and findings are fictional. This packet supports a simulated human review; it is not a real vendor decision or assurance opinion.

This is original fictional training text, not an AICPA SOC 2 report, examination, auditor's opinion, or representation of a real service organization's controls. No auditor or CPA is named or simulated.

Exhibit facts

  • Purported period: 1 January–30 September 2026.
  • Purported report type: Type II-style description for the exercise. The actual report, opinion, criteria, tests, samples, results, and exceptions schedule are not supplied.
  • E02 v1 boundary: Customer support portal. It explicitly excludes payroll processing.
  • E02-v2 lab revision: The synthetic scope field now lists customer support portal and payroll processing, and no excluded services. This is a field-level workflow retest only; it does not create an actual report or cure the missing report contents.
  • Opinion: Not provided.
  • Exceptions: Not represented in this short exhibit; unknown, not “none.”
  • Subservice organizations: Method (inclusive or carve-out), names, services and complementary subservice-organization controls are not provided.
  • CUECs: No actual report schedule is supplied. The separately modelled E03 customer MFA requirement is a proposed case control, not a claim that a real SOC report listed it.

How to reason about it

Type II assurance ordinarily concerns whether described controls were suitably designed and operated over a specified period, as assessed in an actual report. A summary label and date range do not establish that such an examination occurred. The date range is historical and ends 30 September 2026; it does not on its own establish conditions after that date. First obtain the genuine report under appropriate confidentiality controls, then examine the service/system description, criteria, auditor's opinion, test procedures and results, exceptions and management responses, subservice-organization treatment, and CUECs.

For v1, scope mismatch is decisive: a support-portal summary cannot support a conclusion about the payroll service merely because both are associated with the same hypothetical supplier. For v2, a changed declaration makes the fixture eligible for a simulated scope-field check only. It leaves authenticity, actual scope language, period coverage, exceptions, report opinion and control testing unresolved. Do not describe either version as passing a real SOC review.
